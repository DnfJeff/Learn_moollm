# life — section overview

**This is the life-section README. What lives here, how it's organized, and notable changes
are noted here.**

Practical knowledge for running a life. The reader is always **future-you**: the person who
will face this repair, this decision, this recipe again in three years and wants the answer
and the reasoning, not a search through old chats. Personal *records* (appointments, balances)
stay out of the library; durable *understanding* files here.

## Subclasses
- [`health/`](health/README.md) — body and mind, maintained
- [`finance/`](finance/README.md) — money, understood; decisions with rationale
- [`home/`](home/README.md) — dwelling, vehicles, tools, repairs
- [`food/`](food/README.md) — technique, ingredients, keeper recipes

## How to use this section
- **Browsing:** this file, then `SKILL.md` for the ledger.
- **Routing (agent):** `CARD.yml` decides whether new work belongs here.
- **Filing:** copy `../../_templates/unit/`, fill it, register it.
