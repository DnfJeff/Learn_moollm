## The character: Danny Williams

Danny Williams is a successful nightclub entertainer whose act keeps him on the road and whose
family keeps making room for him when he returns — the premise the original title, *Make Room
for Daddy*, states outright. The comedy runs on the gap between the polished performer on
stage and the excitable, easily flustered father at home. The Williams household anchored the
show through cast changes across its 1953–1964 run.

## The performer behind him

Danny Thomas (1912–1991), born Amos Muzyad Yakhoob Kairouz to Lebanese immigrant parents, came
up through nightclubs — the profession the show wrote into its premise. Beyond performing he
became one of early television's significant producers (Danny Thomas Productions, with Sheldon
Leonard), in whose orbit *The Andy Griffith Show* and *The Dick Van Dyke Show* emerged, and he
founded St. Jude Children's Research Hospital (1962), the philanthropy he considered his
life's work. His daughter Marlo Thomas became a television star in her own right (*That Girl*).
