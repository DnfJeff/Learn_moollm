# The Danny Thomas Show

## The work

**Make Room for Daddy** premiered on ABC in 1953, was retitled **The Danny Thomas Show** in
its fourth season, moved to CBS in 1957, and ran through 1964 — eleven seasons in the era when
the filmed, syndicatable sitcom was displacing live broadcast. Danny Thomas starred as **Danny
Williams**, a nightclub entertainer whose work-vs-family tension gave the show its premise and
its title: making room for a father who was always on the road.

The show is also a hinge in sitcom history for what it spun off and connected to: its
production orbit (Danny Thomas Productions, with Sheldon Leonard) seeded *The Andy Griffith
Show* — Sheriff Andy Taylor debuted in a 1960 Danny Thomas Show episode, one of television's
early backdoor pilots — and later *The Dick Van Dyke Show*.
