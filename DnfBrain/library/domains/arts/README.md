# arts — section overview

**This is the arts-section README. What lives here, how it's organized, and notable changes
are noted here.**

Creative works across every medium — the works themselves, their history, criticism, and their
makers. The organizing idea: **the path carries the classification.** By the time you reach
`film/tv/1950s/sitcom/the-danny-thomas-show/characters/danny-thomas.md`, the address has
already told you the medium, the era, the genre, the work, and the kind of material — before
a single file opens.

## Subclasses
- [`film/`](film/README.md) — moving pictures: [`tv/`](film/tv/README.md) (by decade → genre →
  show) and [`cinema/`](film/cinema/README.md)
- [`music/`](music/README.md) — works, artists, genres, theory
- [`literature/`](literature/README.md) — written works and their authors
- [`visual-arts/`](visual-arts/README.md) — painting, photography, design

*(Deeper levels are staged down the exemplar path and otherwise created when their first unit
lands — flat-to-structured, never empty ceremony.)*

## How to use this section
- **Browsing:** read this file, then `SKILL.md` for the full ledger of units.
- **Routing (agent):** `CARD.yml` decides whether new work belongs here.
- **Filing:** walk the spine to the shelf, copy `../../_templates/unit/`, register it.
- **Characters:** full write-ups live inside their work; the
  [characters registry](../../characters/README.md) holds the quick cards.
