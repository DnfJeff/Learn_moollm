# humanities — section overview

**This is the humanities-section README. What lives here, how it's organized, and notable
changes are noted here.**

Durable understanding of human affairs: what happened, what people have thought, and the
languages it happened in. The boundary that matters most here: a person known chiefly through
a creative work (an actor, a showrunner, a character) is filed **with the work** under `arts/`
— this section holds figures whose significance stands apart from any one work. The
[characters registry](../../characters/README.md) resolves by-name lookups either way.

## Subclasses
- [`history/`](history/README.md) — events, eras, figures
- [`philosophy/`](philosophy/README.md) — ideas, arguments, thinkers
- [`language/`](language/README.md) — languages studied, linguistics

## How to use this section
- **Browsing:** this file, then `SKILL.md` for the ledger.
- **Routing (agent):** `CARD.yml` decides whether new work belongs here.
- **Filing:** copy `../../_templates/unit/`, fill it, register it.
