# 🧠 DnfBrain — Front Desk

Welcome to the brain space. This is the landing note; open it first. Everything below is one
click away, and the graph view will show it all linked.

> **How to find things here:** read the cheapest thing that answers your question, and never
> open a deeper level without the one above it. Front desk → the directory → the right
> section → the shelf → the note. The path itself carries the classification — by the time
> you reach `arts/film/tv/1950s/sitcom/the-danny-thomas-show/`, you already know what you're
> holding.

## Start here

- **[The directory](library/INDEX.md)** — the chief librarian: what every section holds and
  how to reach it. Read this to decide *where* something lives.
- **[The inbox](library/inbox/README.md)** — drop anything unsorted here. No filing required
  at the door; triage happens on a rhythm.
- **[Characters](library/characters/README.md)** — the by-name registry: look a figure up by
  name; the card routes to every full write-up they've earned.

## The sections (permanent knowledge)

- **[arts](library/domains/arts/README.md)** — creative works and their makers: film, tv,
  music, literature, visual art.
- **[tech](library/domains/tech/README.md)** — computing down to the internals: pc & gaming,
  programming, ai, the web.
- **[science](library/domains/science/README.md)** — the natural and formal worlds.
- **[humanities](library/domains/humanities/README.md)** — history, philosophy, language.
- **[life](library/domains/life/README.md)** — health, finance, home, food — for future-you.
- **[practices](library/domains/practices/README.md)** — repeatable ways of working.

Each section has a **librarian** (its `SKILL.md`) that lists everything on the shelf and knows
what belongs there. Ask it "where does this go?" before you file.

## Work in progress

- **[projects](library/projects/README.md)** — time-bound initiatives. Announce a new one and
  the `project-intake` capability opens it wired to the shelves it draws on; when it turns up
  something reusable, that graduates into a section above.

## Keeping it usable

- **[The ontology](library/meta/ontology.md)** — the controlled vocabulary. Tags come from
  here, coined deliberately, or they turn to noise.
- **[Change log](library/meta/structure-log.md)** — every structural change to the library,
  with the reason. Append-only.
- **[The templates](library/_templates/README.md)** — copy-me card stacks for a new section, a
  new note, a new project, or a new registry.

## The one rule that keeps this alive

Capture is free; filing is deliberate. Put anything in the **inbox** the moment you have it,
and let the librarians place it later. A perfect empty system helps no one; a slightly messy
inbox that always gets triaged is the whole game.
